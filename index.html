Try it: an inverting amplifier is loaded. Click the 2 V label and enter 5 to watch the op-amp clip at its rail, or pick Resistor and click two grid dots to add a part. Results update instantly.

Click a start dot, then an end dot to place two-terminal parts (V source: + at the end; I source arrow points to the end). Ground and op-amp take a single click. Wires join any part end lying on them. Click a value or the op-amp's rails label to edit it (SI suffixes OK: 4.7k, 2m).

KCL Recipe

  1. Pick ground (0 V), label node voltages.
  2. Each branch: i = (Vstart − Vend)/R.
  3. At each node: Σ ileaving = 0.
  4. Ideal op-amp: V+ = V−, no input current.
