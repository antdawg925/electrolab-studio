How to use: pick a part, click a start grid dot then an end dot (V source: + goes at the end; I source arrow points to the end). Ground & op-amp: single click. Click a value label to edit it (4.7k, 2m OK). Wires connect at their ends and to any part end lying on them. Drag with Move, delete with right-click or Delete. Keys: R V I W G O M X, Esc cancels, Ctrl+Z undoes. Solves automatically.
